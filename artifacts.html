02 Imaginary Worlds

An in-person charity art event.

IMAGINARY WORLDS is a participatory art event in which children, families, and community members make alternate places together: tiny architectures, impossible maps, invented creatures, hopeful futures, and rooms for what the real world has not yet made space for.

Each edition supports a chosen community cause. Making becomes a way to gather attention, raise funds, and rehearse possibility.

  • Partner cause — chosen per edition
  • Event documentation — photographed as archive
  • Participant-created artworks — shown with care
  • Amount raised — reported when applicable
  • Participant reflections — in their own words

03 Inner Landscapes

An in-person art night.

INNER LANDSCAPES is an evening studio for adults and older teens. Through drawing, collage, gentle writing, sound, and shared prompts, participants map the emotional terrain beneath the surface of daily life.

No artistic confidence is required. This is a space for process, not performance — for people who want to return to their inner world through materials.
