About the studio

REMIXED TABLE is an art and learning studio for children, families, educators, and curious adults.

We turn learning into a studio without walls — a place where art gives shape to feeling, wellness becomes a practice of attention, and children and adults are trusted to make meaning together.

We work with materials, stories, food, questions, and conversation. We make room for the unfinished, and treat creativity not as an extra but as a way of paying attention and caring for one another.

Field noteFounder · In the studio

The founder

REMIXED TABLE is led by an artist and educator working at the intersection of art, family life, and learning. The studio grew out of years spent teaching, making, parenting, and paying close attention to how people — especially children — come to notice what matters.

The work draws on time in classrooms and international-school settings, a sustained studio practice, and a belief that imagination deserves to be taken seriously at every age.
