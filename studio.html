01 Manifesto

A manifesto for the inner classroom.

Come to the table. Bring what you notice.

We believe education is more than instruction. It is a way of meeting the world.

REMIXED TABLE turns learning into a studio without walls: a place where art gives shape to feeling, wellness becomes a practice of attention, and children and adults are trusted to make meaning together.

We work with materials, stories, food, questions, movement, memory, and conversation. We make room for the unfinished. We believe a sketchbook can be a thinking tool, a shared meal can be a curriculum, and an ordinary afternoon can become a site of discovery.

We are here for children who ask difficult questions, parents who want more connection and less performance, educators who want to teach with imagination, and adults who have not forgotten the feeling of making something by hand.

We do not treat creativity as an extra. We treat it as a way of paying attention, caring for one another, and practicing agency in the present moment.

03 The Table

Not only furniture.

The table is a place where ideas become physical: a surface for drawing, feeding, arguing, listening, spilling, revising, and beginning again.

Our logo is a table for a reason. It holds the shared-table philosophy of REMIXED TABLE — the role of food, conversation, art, and community in everything we make. Come alone, with a child, with a collaborator, or with a question.

A small table. A large idea.

04 People

Field noteFounder · In the studio

The founder

REMIXED TABLE is led by an artist and educator working at the intersection of art, family life, and learning. The studio grew out of years spent teaching, making, parenting, and paying close attention to how people — especially children — come to notice what matters.

The work draws on time in classrooms and international-school settings, a sustained studio practice, and a belief that imagination deserves to be taken seriously at every age.

Collaborators & visiting voices

We work alongside visiting artists, educators, and partners — cafés, studios, community spaces, charities, schools, and wellness practitioners — who share a commitment to making with care.
