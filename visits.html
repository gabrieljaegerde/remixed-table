Inner Landscapes — Adult Art Night

An evening to map the emotional terrain beneath an ordinary week — through drawing, collage, gentle writing, and shared prompts.

Date
Saturday, 12 September 2026
Time
7:00 – 9:30 PM
Location
Nomadic · shared on booking
Age / audience
Adults & older teens (16+)
Price
$38 USD · materials included
Availability
4 of 12 seats left
Reserve a place →

What you'll do

Work through three guided prompts at your own pace, moving between drawing, collage, and quiet writing. No critique, no sharing required.

What you'll leave with

A small body of process work, a take-home prompt, and a calmer relationship to the blank page.

Who it's for

Adults and older teens. No artistic confidence required — this is a space for process, not performance.

What's included

All materials, tea and light refreshments, and a facilitated, unhurried evening.

Accessibility

Step-free studio access. Please tell us about any access needs, allergies, or dietary requirements in the booking form and we'll prepare accordingly.

Facilitator

Led by the REMIXED TABLE founder, an artist and educator working nomadically.

Cancellation policy

Full refund up to 72 hours before the session; a credit toward a future visit thereafter.
